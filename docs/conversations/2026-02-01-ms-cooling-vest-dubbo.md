### What this one is good at

The **Ergodyne Chill-Its 6685 "dry evaporative" vest** (the one you screenshotted on Blackwoods) is:

- **light + low-fuss** (no gel/ice packs)
- **great in Dubbo's dry heat** *if there's airflow* (walking outside, breeze/fan)
- Fill with **400-600 mL water**, can cool **up to ~10°C below ambient**, and can last **up to 3 days** depending on conditions; **humidity reduces performance**.

Source: Pryme Australia, *Chill-Its 6685 Premium Dry Evaporative Cooling Vest*  
https://pryme.com.au/product/chill-its-6685-premium-dry-evaporative-cooling-vest/

So: **excellent for outdoor dry heat + airflow**, *less ideal* for still air / indoors / under layers.

### What's often better for MS, especially for reliability

For MS, a lot of people prefer **phase-change pack vests** because they give **steady, predictable cooling** for a few hours (no reliance on evaporation/airflow). Example: a phase-change vest that maintains about **18°C for up to ~4 hours**.

Source: Pryme Australia, *Chill-Its 6215 Premium FR Phase Change Cooling Vest with Packs*  
https://pryme.com.au/product/chill-its-6215-premium-fr-phase-change-cooling-vest-with-packs/

There are also phase-change systems available in different target temperatures, for example **6.5°C / 15°C / 21°C / 24°C**.

Source: Cool Performance Australia, *Cooling for Medical Need*  
https://cool-performance.com.au/cooling-for-medical-need

MS Australia notes cooling vests can help movement/fatigue, but **ice/gel-pack styles can be heavy** and once warmed can **trap heat**.

Source: MS Australia, *Heat Sensitivity and MS*  
https://www.msaustralia.org.au/news/heat-sensitivity-ms/

And the research overall basically says: cooling garments help, but **no single type is clearly "the best" — people need to try what suits them**.

Source: ScienceDirect article  
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2211034823004133

### Simple decision

- **Mostly outdoors in Dubbo heat + she wants light and comfy:** the 6685 evaporative vest is a solid pick.
- **She needs reliable cooling for shopping/errands/driving/walking, including lower-airflow situations:** look at a **phase-change vest (15-18°C) + a spare set of packs**.
